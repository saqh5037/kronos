# Redesign direction

This is the brief the rebuild works from. It keeps the product truth, the data model and the identity; it replaces the information architecture, the shell, the component layer and the rules that were being broken. Mode per surface: athlete app and admin are **Operate** (task completion, scanability, consistency); landing is **Persuade**; TV is **Experience**.

## 1. Principles (the rules, with the failure each one prevents)

| #   | Principle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Prevents (from the audit)                                                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --- | ------- | ---- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **One anchor, one action per screen.** The first viewport answers "what is happening" and offers one primary action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Athlete home with zero actions and today's WOD 1,100 px down; check-in at y≈1,760 under analytics |
| 2   | **Progressive disclosure.** Number → 7/30-day trend with delta → raw list. | 3   | **Semantic colour only.** Lime is the brand and the "good" state. Orange means a real warning, red a real error. Intensity is opacity of lime, never a hue change. | 4   | **One shell per surface, one app bar, one icon set.** Admin renders one `AdminShell` from the layout for every role and route. ## 3. Information architecture

### Athlete app (PWA; 360 primary; 720 px centred column on desktop)

Five tabs: **Hoy · WOD · Reservar · Progreso · Yo**. Skills lives under Progreso until the athlete has an active progression. Salud disappears as a tab: its content becomes the Estado card on Hoy and body metrics under Progreso.

| Screen       | First viewport                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Below the fold                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Notes                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hoy**      | Estado del día (one number 0–100, three bands: Listo / Moderado / Recupera, provenance line) · Today's WOD name + type + your class time or "sin clase" · **one lime CTA** ("Reservar 18:00" or "Registrar Murph") | Streak tile and next-PR tile (two `StatTile`s) · AI briefing (Gemini greeting already exists; make it about today) · this week strip · box leaderboard for today                                                     | Badges leave the home. Cancelling a class lives inside the class card, never as the first button |
| **WOD**      | Today's WOD, one structured list (no duplicated free text), cap and score type · sticky "Registrar" bar                                                                                                            | Type-aware form: mm:ss mask for time, kg stepper for load, reps counter; "cap alcanzado → reps" toggle; RX / Escalado segmented control with one info sheet; "Tu último Murph: 41:20 RX" autofill · then leaderboard | Photo-WOD and quick-WOD (personal box) as secondary actions in the app bar                       |
| **Reservar** | Day strip · booked summary ("Mañana 06:00 · Lobo") · list of today's classes with one-tap Reservar                                                                                                                 | Filters in a `Sheet`, not stacked selects · past classes show "Ver resultados", never a booking button                                                                                                               | Waitlist state and promotion visible on the card                                                 |
| **Progreso** | Three `StatTile`s: asistencia, fuerza, cardio (big number, 7-day sparkline, delta)                                                                                                                                 | Tap → 30/90-day chart → raw list · PRs by movement · skills progression · badges (secondary, unlocked ≠ locked) · body metrics                                                                                       | Kills the normalised kg + mm:ss line and the radar with zeros                                    |
| **Yo**       | Identity (real name), membership and next charge                                                                                                                                                                   | Payments and receipts · notifications · devices (Whoop connect, Oura later) · privacy · help                                                                                                                         | The Whoop connection lives here; the Recovery card lives on Hoy                                  |

**Estado del día (spec)**. Inputs: (a) Whoop recovery % and sleep performance when connected; (b) the existing readiness/RPE survey answer for today; (c) 7-day training load from scores and attendance (tonnage and adherence analytics already exist). Output: 0–100, band, a one-line recommendation for today's WOD (RX / Escalado / Técnica) and the provenance. Without any input for today, the card asks the two-tap survey. This is the "nothing to envy Whoop" move: credible without hardware, better with it, and it knows the workout.

### Public

- `/` stays a router but renders visible on SSR; `/box` is rewritten from the truth table (shipped / en piloto / roadmap), one trial, two CTAs ("Empezar prueba", "Hablar por WhatsApp"), real screenshots from the rebuilt app, real pilot numbers; `/atletas` keeps only what can be shown.
- Legal: a Mexican "Aviso de Privacidad" (LFPDPPP, derechos ARCO) and Terms that match the product.
- Invitation and error states carry the box name, the coach's contact and one action.
- TV mode is white-label: the box's logo and colour first, Kronos as a footer mark; lime-opacity ranks; sized to content.

## 4. The five screens that define the rebuild

1. **Athlete · Hoy.** Estado ring (lime, band label), WOD card with class time and one CTA, then two tiles. This is the screenshot that goes on the landing.
2. **Athlete · WOD + registro.** One structured workout, sticky CTA, type-aware form, autofill of the last result, leaderboard after saving with "tu posición" pinned.
3. **Coach · Hoy on a phone.** Next class card expanded: roster with 44 px toggles, "Guardar (4)" in a fixed bottom bar, "Cargar pizarra" as the secondary action.
4. **Owner · Hoy on desktop.** MRR, cobros del periodo, asistencia, morosos, at-risk, all from one summary with the period label; deltas coloured by sign; next classes sorted.
5. **Admin · Dinero on a phone.** Row cards with amount right-aligned in Plex Mono, status chip, one primary "Registrar cobro" in the first viewport, morosos with "Recordar por WhatsApp".

## 5. Copy and language rules

- Neutral Mexican Spanish for all product copy. Owners think in "mensualidades", "morosos", "cuántos vinieron hoy", not MRR / churn / tier / slug.
- CrossFit jargon stays in English where the community uses it (WOD, RX, AMRAP, EMOM, For Time) and nowhere else.
- Enum → label dictionary at the presentation boundary (`ATTENDED → Asistió`, `BOOKED → Reservado`, `PAID → Pagado`, `UNLIMITED → Ilimitado`, `ROUNDS_REPS → Rondas + reps`).
- Numbers: `Intl.NumberFormat("es-MX")` everywhere, currency as `$2,500 MXN`, dates as `15 sep` / `mar 15 sep`, times 24 h across admin.
- No vendor names in user copy ("análisis con IA", not "Gemini Vision").

## 6. What stays as-is

WOD + form on one screen; the skill progression ladder; the week grid; PR cards; the utilization heatmap; the whiteboard OCR three-step flow; the onboarding wizard tone; the MX-native lead form; the movement coaching content (translated); the streak hero; the TV concept; the personal-box mode (no new investment).
