# Gym / CrossFit Box SaaS Benchmark — September 2026

**Method.** Official pricing/feature pages, vendor release notes, App Store listings, Capterra/Software Advice reviews (2025–26), and a July 2026 Reddit round-up ([vibefam](https://vibefam.com/gym-management-software-reddit-2026/)) because reddit.com blocks direct crawling. `help.wodify.com` and `help.sugarwod.com` refused fetches; their items come from search snippets of the linked articles. Anything I could not confirm from a primary source is tagged **UNVERIFIED**. Two circulating claims are false: SugarWOD was _not_ bought by PushPress (2022) or Wodify (2023); [Daxko has owned it since 2019](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/daxko-acquires-sugarwod-to-become-the-unrivaled-software-provider-for-affiliate-gyms-300851711.html).

## 1. Wodify

**Positioning.** The all-in-one incumbent "built for CrossFit from day one" (2012), now selling to CrossFit, jiu-jitsu and boutique studios; ~5,000 businesses, single boxes up to multi-location chains, US-centric ([wodify.com/solutions](https://www.wodify.com/solutions/crossfit-functional-fitness)).

**Pricing 2026.** The [pricing page](https://www.wodify.com/pricing) now shows "starting at ~~$199~~ $99/month per location," a "book a demo by September 30" promo worth "$100/month off for life"; PricingSaaS recorded Essentials at [$179/mo](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/wodify) in 2026. The $79 quoted by [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/wodify/pricing)/Capterra is stale — a large 2026 list-price increase. Accelerate/Ultimate: quote only. Add-ons: branded app [$79/mo + Apple dev account](https://www.wodify.com/blog/wodify-faqs); performance tracking (Perform), Pulse heart-rate, website, marketplace: unpriced. Processing "as low as" 2.7% + 30¢ card / 1.5% + 30¢ ACH (FAQ says 2.6% + 25¢). Month-to-month; undisclosed onboarding fee.

**Shipped last 12 months.** [Aug 8, 2025](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/34094091963927-What-s-New-August-8th-2025)/[Sep 5, 2025](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/34780407417751-What-s-New-September-5th-2025): variable reps/sets, POS + Mobile Retail full launch, social share. [Dec 8, 2025 year-in-review](https://www.wodify.com/blog/behind-the-numbers-year-in-review-2): Online Sales Page, Sites, refreshed Appointments, Automations, unified Inbox. [Jan 9, 2026](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/37530212119575-What-s-New-January-9th-2026): admin MFA, mass membership repricing, per-class no-show/late-cancel policies. [Jan 23](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/37884827378071-What-s-New-January-23rd-2026): no-show marking on Kiosk+. [Feb 20](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/38501400858903-What-s-New-February-20th-2026): drop-in limits per class. [May 2](https://help.wodify.com/hc/en-us/articles/40127425231127-What-s-New-May-2nd-2026): scheduled workout publishing, progression certificates, client deletion. AI: [Ask Wodify](https://www.wodify.com/products/ai-solutions-for-gyms-and-fitness) (natural-language business insights, "included with every plan" on credits), AI email/site copy assistant, and the older [Retain](https://www.wodify.com/use-cases/retain-clients) attendance-based churn model; a third-party "AI at-risk added late 2025" claim is **UNVERIFIED**.

**Complaints (2025–26).** [Capterra 4.5/154](https://www.capterra.com/p/159663/Wodify/reviews/): "price… too high for some markets" (Apr 2025); "communication is probably the worst of any company" (Apr 2025); "glitchy when there are updates" (Mar 2025); "member app is sometimes slow and often needs to be un/reinstalled" (Mar 2025); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/gym-management/wodify-core-profile/): rigid membership periods, slow chat. Reddit: CrossFit DNA "feels awkward for HYROX, powerlifting, or open-gym." **Loved:** all-in-one consolidation, fast support ("Lightning fast customer service," May 2025), long-tenure loyalty ("on Wodify since 2012… I LOVE IT").

**Mexico/LatAm fit.** Weak. App languages are English, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Portuguese — [no Spanish](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wodify/id1563729830). USD-only pricing, Stripe-only rails, SMS "in the U.S. and Canada" only ([FAQ](https://www.wodify.com/blog/wodify-faqs)); support "principalmente en inglés" per LatAm competitor [Crossfy](https://www.crossfyapp.com/comparar/wodify). No named Mexican boxes found.

## 2. SugarWOD (Daxko / Zen Planner)

**Positioning.** The workout-experience layer for affiliates (WOD publishing, whiteboard, community), explicitly _not_ gym operations — "virtually all customers" pair it with Zen Planner, PushPress, Mindbody or Pike13 ([help](https://help.sugarwod.com/hc/en-us/articles/235804808-Does-SugarWOD-do-gym-operations-)). 500k+ athletes, global.

**Pricing 2026.** Per registered athlete, no contract, tiers beyond 250 by quote ([pricing](https://www.sugarwod.com/pricing/), amounts are an image). Secondary source lists ≤50 athletes $29, 51–100 $69, 101–200 $119, 201–300 $179, 301–400 $219 ([heartwellness](https://heartwellness.app/knowledge/how-much-does-sugarwod-cost)) — **UNVERIFIED** exact figures. Free for independent athletes; 2-week gym trial.

**Shipped last 12 months.** [State of the Nation, Jan 27, 2026](https://www.sugarwod.com/2026/01/state-of-the-nation-whats-new-and-whats-next-for-zen-planner-and-sugarwod/): 2025 delivered a cleaner mobile workout view, community feed (11k monthly engagements vs 700 in 2024), threaded coach–athlete comments, shareable/private notes; 2026 roadmap = redesigned profiles, easier gym/subscription switching, GPS run tracking. GPS runs with Apple Health sync and Live Activities are live in the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sugarwod/id665516348) (v10.3.1). A mid-2026 claim of "30 feature releases, incidents −50%, bugs −52%" is **UNVERIFIED** (page unfetchable). Zen Planner side: Staff App, Engage AI lead follow-up.

**Complaints.** App Store: freezes/slow loads, daily forced logouts with unresponsive support, updates removed the history shortcut and 1RM charts. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/229043/SugarWOD/): "Super ambiguous price increases happening multiple times within a year" (Feb 2024), support "as productive as pissing into the wind," "not as user friendly as beyond the whiteboard." **Loved:** dead-simple logging, PR history, fist-bumps/community, BoxTV, Zen Planner sync.

**Mexico/LatAm fit.** Best of the incumbents on language: [Spanish UI since 2021](https://www.sugarwod.com/2022/02/what-sugarwod-accomplished-in-2021/) (device-language based). No billing, so no payment-rail issue — but a Mexican box still needs a separate Spanish billing tool.

## 3. BTWB (Beyond the Whiteboard)

**Positioning.** Data-heavy tracking for athletes and affiliates ("Fitness Level" 1–100, imbalances), with bundled affiliate programming from Mayhem/PRVN/Linchpin; US-centric, small team ([home](https://beyondthewhiteboard.com/)).

**Pricing.** Athletes $7.99/mo or $87/yr; gyms billed monthly by member-count tier (amounts unpublished — **UNVERIFIED**) or "Member Pays" mode; 30-day gym trial ([support, last updated Sep 2024](https://support.btwb.com/en/support/solutions/articles/35000014855-how-much-does-btwb-cost-)).

**Shipped.** No dated changelog exists (blog's only 2025 post is non-product). Current site advertises an [AI Planner](https://plan.btwb.com/) ("first of many AI features," parses pasted workout text using 100M+ results), AI logging, Tempus timer on phone and Apple Watch with HR, Workout TV via Chromecast, macros tracker; the homepage now also lists member billing, class scheduling and waivers — depth **UNVERIFIED**. App v10.5.201 "bug fixes."

**Complaints.** [App Store 4.6/1.5k](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/btwb-wod-tracking/id583688662): redesign added "at least two more clicks," font "too small to use," clunky percentage calculator. [GGR](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/beyond-the-whiteboard-review): overwhelming at first, "not ideal for smaller gyms." **Loved:** depth of analytics, Fitness Level, cheap per-athlete price, programming bundles.

**Mexico/LatAm fit.** English-only app; USD only. Not a contender for Spanish-first boxes.

## 4. PushPress

**Positioning.** "Built by gym owners" challenger for CrossFit/functional-fitness affiliates of 50–300 members; 5,000+ gyms, 34 countries, $650M+ annual payment volume on Stripe ([Stripe case study](https://stripe.com/ae/customers/pushpress)); raised $20M; "AI-first" narrative.

**Pricing 2026** ([pricing](https://www.pushpress.com/pricing)). Core Free $0 (4.99% + 30¢ card, 48-hour support SLA); Pro $159/mo (2.89% + 30¢ card, 0.79% + 30¢ ACH); Max $229 (2.75% + 30¢). Train from $79/mo (20 clients), Grow CRM $329/mo, branded app $81–97/mo; full stack ≈ $559/mo. "Price in USD. Contact Sales for international pricing."

**Shipped last 12 months** ([Dec 18, 2025 recap](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/2025-the-year-pushpress-hit-a-pr), [Wrapped 2025](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/pushpress-wrapped-2025)): AI Assistant controlled beta (Pro/Max; charges no-shows, books classes, edits memberships, answers data questions — [does not generate programming](https://www.pushpress.com/features/ai-assistant)), Tap to Pay via Stripe reader, Kiosk Mode, martial-arts rank tracking, invite-only events, Staff app chat rebuild, plans/billing redesign, People redesign, Members app celebration posts, Screens showing [live Train results](https://help.pushpress.com/en/articles/11888170-display-workout-results-in-the-screens-app-by-pushpress); Unified Inbox teased for 2026. "100+ features in 2025."

**Complaints.** [Capterra 4.7/186](https://www.capterra.com/p/172781/PushPress/reviews/): "reporting and analytics could be more detailed," "mobile app can occasionally feel slow" (Jun 2026), pushy sales (Oct 2025). [Gymdesk, Jun 2026](https://gymdesk.com/blog/is-pushpress-good-for-gyms): billing/password emails not reaching members, "billing is also erratic… members being re-charged," modular "all-in-one." Reddit 2026: "Glitchy af, and gets real expensive real fast"; "constantly down or breaking when they release new features." [Members app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pushpress-member-portal/id1309909578): buried lifting history, crashing calculator, users buy SugarWOD/Komodo for progress. **Loved:** ease of use and staff training, support quality (G2 9.6), Train's PR confetti/leaderboards, free migration from Wodify/Mindbody/Zen Planner.

**Mexico/LatAm fit.** English-only app; USD pricing; Stripe-only; no LatAm content in the Stripe study. Some South America staff exist (**UNVERIFIED** relevance).

## 5. TrainHeroic

**Positioning.** Coach-to-athlete strength programming marketplace (teams, S&C, remote coaching) — not gym management. Acquired with TrainingPeaks by **Garmin on July 22, 2026**; no roadmap disclosed, third-party device access in question ([DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2026/07/garmin-acquires-training-trainheroic.html)).

**Pricing** ([coach page](https://www.trainheroic.com/coach/)): $9.99 (1 athlete) → $34.99 (15) → $99.99 (100) → $399.99 (1,000); assistant coaches $9.99 each; marketplace revenue share undisclosed.

**Shipped.** Nutrition habit tracking in Athlete Pro (late 2025, secondary source). No AI, no billing/scheduling. **Complaints** ([Coachbox, Jul 2026](https://coachbox.app/en/compare/trainheroic-review/)): weak endurance/wearable data flow, minimal messaging. **Loved:** percentage/RPE auto-scaling programming, exercise video library, team leaderboards, clean athlete app.

**Mexico/LatAm fit.** Irrelevant for box ops; English app, USD.

## Gaps and opportunities

**(a) Table stakes in 2026.** Recurring billing with retry logic and family/shared payments; class booking with auto-waitlist and per-class no-show/late-cancel policies; kiosk check-in; a native athlete app (branded optional) with WOD view, score logging, auto-PR detection, benchmark history; a TV whiteboard/leaderboard synced in real time; coach roster + notes; push notifications and email; digital waivers; basic dashboards (attendance, revenue, churn); Zapier/API; and _some_ AI copy (Wodify, PushPress, Zen Planner all ship an assistant). Kronos already covers most of this list; the visible holes versus incumbents are per-class cancellation policies, drop-in caps, waivers, and a TV display mode.

**(b) Differentiators few have.** Native wearable ingestion beyond Myzone belts (nobody does Whoop/Garmin; SugarWOD only Apple Health for runs); AI that _programs_ (only BTWB's parser and Crossfy claim it — PushPress explicitly does not); competition/throwdown module with heats and live scoring (Wodify Arena is the only one, and it is a separate product); real streak/badge gamification (Wodify streaks and certificates; SugarWOD refuses on principle); integrated performance tracking _without_ a paid add-on (Wodify gates leaderboards behind Perform, PushPress behind Train +$79); multi-tenant admin for small chains without "enterprise" pricing.

**(c) Where incumbents are weak per reviews.** Reliability on release ("constantly down or breaking when they release new features," "glitchy when there are updates"); billing correctness (double charges, re-charges, invoices not delivered); opaque and rising pricing (Wodify's 2026 jump to ~$179–199 list, SugarWOD's "multiple increases within a year," PushPress's $559 real stack); modular "all-in-one" that is not; app UX regressions (BTWB clicks, SugarWOD removing 1RM charts, PushPress burying history); reporting depth (PushPress); support that degrades post-sale (PE-owned platforms). A new entrant wins on boring excellence: transparent flat price, no add-on gating, a changelog with regression tests, and a stable app.

**(d) Unserved for Mexico/LatAm boxes.**

1. **Language + currency.** None of Wodify, PushPress, BTWB ship Spanish; all price in USD and settle via Stripe; Wodify SMS is US/CA only. Only SugarWOD (tracking-only) is localized.
2. **Payment rails.** Zero global players support Mercado Pago, Conekta, OpenPay, OXXO cash-in or SPEI; local players either do one rail (Mercado Pago) or push manual entry. A box needs card recurring + OXXO/SPEI references + a cash ledger with reconciliation and delinquency control in one flow, plus CFDI invoicing (no vendor surfaced it — worth confirming demand).
3. **WhatsApp as the primary channel.** Klasius and GymGestión prove demand (reminders, payment links, natural-language booking); incumbents route WhatsApp only through PushPress Grow at $329/mo. WhatsApp Business API templates for class reminders, waitlist promotion, failed-payment recovery and PR announcements is an obvious wedge.
4. **Local price points.** LatAm competitors cluster at $200–$1,500 MXN/mo, flat, no commissions; Wodify/PushPress effective stacks are $250–$560 USD. A tiered MXN price (roughly $499–1,999 MXN) with no per-feature add-ons undercuts both.
5. **CrossFit depth in Spanish.** Klasius has no app/leaderboard, Boxmagic/Fitco/GymGestión are generic studios; only Crossfy is box-specific. Combining SugarWOD-grade whiteboard/PR tracking with Mexican rails, WhatsApp and Spanish-first UX is a space no one occupies today.
